# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Technical and Concise**: Documentation and UI text should prioritize clarity and brevity. Focus on providing the necessary information without unnecessary elaboration, keeping it efficient for developers and experienced users.

## Brand Messaging
- **Utilitarian and Reliable**: The application should feel like a dependable tool. The core message is "It just works, and your data is safe," emphasizing functionality and privacy above all else.

## Visual Identity
- **Minimalism**: The UI should be unobtrusive, placing the primary focus on the map and the GPX data. Avoid clutter and unnecessary decorative elements.
- **Clarity**: Ensure high readability through the use of high-contrast elements and clean, legible typography. The data should be easy to interpret at a glance.
